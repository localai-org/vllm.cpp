Asserting the NEW wording would only move the pin from `(owed` to `RAN 2026-08-18, PASS`, and the rerun this row already owes under [#1226](https://github.com/mudler/vllm.cpp/issues/1226) against a coarser signature key would red it again. Lead-scoping is measured rather than assumed: a whole-item search credits item 5 with a disposition it does not declare, because its body contains `fail` in the phrase "proving the instrument can fail". Both historical wordings of item 6 are pinned as fixtures, which is what proves the new assertion is state-independent rather than re-pinned to today's sentence. The rule is NOT swept over every gate item and the reason is measured, not assumed: items 1 and 5 yield no command and declare no disposition, so a sweep is red on arrival, and widening the vocabulary until they pass would make it match ordinary English.
