Title: **`FlashAttentionBackend::supports_compute_capability` is upstream's NVIDIA `capability >= (8,0)` (`include/vllm/v1/attention/backend.h:401-404`, `flash_attn.py:200-202`), and `MetalPlatform::get_device_capability` (`src/vllm/platforms/metal.cpp:35-38`) answers with the MTLGPUFamilyApple GENERATION — deliberately, with a comment saying `has_device_capability(N, 0)` means "Apple family >= N". `validate_configuration` at `src/vllm/v1/attention/backend.cpp:197` compares the two whenever `capability.present()`, so an SM-version bar is applied to an Apple family number.** Apple family 9 on the M4 gate box clears it by coincidence; a GitHub `macos-15` runner reports lower, FLASH_ATTN — the ONLY entry in `MetalPlatform::get_attn_backend_priority()` — is refused, and `SelectAttentionBackendName` throws. Until then `macos-metal-mlx` is RED on `main`, deliberately not hidden behind `continue-on-error` or a skip.
